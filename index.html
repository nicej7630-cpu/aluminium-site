Privacy

How we use your details.

What we collect. When you request a quote or send a message, we collect your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, and the details you give us about your project.

Why. We use them only to prepare your quote, reply to your message, and arrange any order you choose to place.

Who sees them. Only Timaya Aluminum Products staff who handle your enquiry. We do not sell or share them. Messages are sent through Web3Forms, which delivers them to our email, and are stored securely in our private database.

How long. We keep them while needed to respond and manage any order, and delete them when you ask us to.

Your choices. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email alumtiy@gmail.com from the address you used.

Cookies. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts.
