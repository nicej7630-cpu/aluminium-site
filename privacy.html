Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 May 2026

This notice explains what information Timaya Aluminum Products collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what choices you have. If anything is unclear, email us at alumtiy@gmail.com.

Who we are

Timaya Aluminum Products manufactures and installs aluminium and PVC windows, doors, frames and roofing. Our office is at 35 Dallimore Street, Ado Ekiti, Nigeria.

What we collect

When you request a trade quote, we collect:

  • Your name, and your company or site name, if you give it
  • Your email address and phone or WhatsApp number
  • The product you need, the approximate quantity, and the site area
  • When you need the work, and any sizes, drawings or other details you include

We do not ask for bank details, passwords, or identity numbers through this website.

Why we collect it

  • To prepare an itemised quote for your project
  • To contact you about that quote, and to answer your questions
  • To arrange production, delivery and installation if you accept a quote

Who can see your details

Only Timaya Aluminum Products staff who handle quotes can see your details. We do not sell them, and we do not share them with advertisers.

Quote requests are sent through an online form service, Web3Forms, which delivers them to our email. That service processes the details only to deliver your request.

How long we keep them

We keep quote requests for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting order. We delete them when you ask us to, unless we need to keep records for accounting or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Ask us what details we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update them
  • Ask us to delete them
  • Tell us to stop contacting you about a quote

To make any of these requests, email alumtiy@gmail.com from the address you used on the form.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the WhatsApp link opens WhatsApp when you choose to use it.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
